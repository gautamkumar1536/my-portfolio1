About Me

I'm a 2nd-year Computer Science & Engineering (IoT) student at Bakhtiyarpur College of Engineering, currently in my 3rd semester. My world runs on two parallel tracks: writing code and connecting hardware to it — and expressing myself through art, dance, and sport.

On the technical side, I build with C, C++, Java, Python and the web stack, while exploring how IoT turns logic into something physical. Off the keyboard, I've won stage and decoration events, raced on the track, and led as a Bharat Scouts & Guides leader in school — experiences that shaped how I lead, plan, and stay disciplined under pressure.

I believe the best engineers stay multi-dimensional — so I keep sketching, dancing, and running, right alongside debugging.
